Lap Doctor Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Lap Doctor is developed by Peter Zielenski. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information stays on your device

Lap Doctor processes precise location, timestamps, speed, and related GPS measurements to detect movement, establish a start line, calculate lap times, and record sessions. Events, session results, notes, settings, and diagnostic logs are stored locally on your device. Lap Doctor does not automatically upload this information to the developer or a developer-operated server.

Location permissions

The app requests precise location access and background location access so recording can continue while the screen is locked or the app is in the background. You can change or revoke these permissions in your device settings. Recording features may not work without the required permissions.

No advertising or tracking

Lap Doctor does not require a user account, display advertising, or include a third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or dedicated crash-reporting service. The developer does not sell your personal information or use your location for advertising.

Exports and sharing

When you choose to export a session, Lap Doctor creates a KML file and opens your device's sharing options. Exported files can contain precise coordinates, timestamps, speed measurements, and event or session names. Share them only with recipients and services you trust.

Exported KML files reference Google-hosted marker icons. A mapping application may contact Google's servers to retrieve those icons when you open the file. Apps and services you choose to open, store, or share exported files have their own privacy practices.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for support.

Managing and deleting data

You can delete events and sessions using the app's controls. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data from the device. Exported files, support emails, and device backups are separate copies and must be managed separately. Device backups may include app data according to your device and backup settings.

Apple services

App Store purchases, TestFlight, device diagnostics, and device backup services are governed by Apple's policies and your settings. Lap Doctor does not receive your payment-card details.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
